# NowTrading Quant V9 Risk Governance Map

This document maps out the risk engine hierarchy, decisions, and guard mechanisms implemented in `RiskGateway` (`src/core/risk_engine.py`) to prevent loss of capital.

## Risk Gateway Decisions

Every trade plan generated by the system must pass through the `RiskGateway` before routing to execution. The gateway issues one of three decisions:

### 1. `ALLOW` (PASS)
- **Behavior:** The trade plan satisfies all market and account safety checks. Sizing multipliers determined by the ML Gatekeeper are respected, and the order is forwarded to the Order Router for execution.

### 2. `SOFT_BLOCK`
- **Behavior:** A minor or transient safety guard has triggered (e.g., spread is too wide, slippage limit exceeded, ATR shock detected, or temporary loss streak limit hit).
- **Outcome:** The signal is blocked. Bots remain active, but execution is suspended until conditions return to nominal.

### 3. `HARD_KILL`
- **Behavior:** A critical risk limit has been breached (e.g., account daily drawdown exceeds the maximum allowed percentage, or weekly drawdown hits maximum limits).
- **Outcome:** Trading is immediately halted across the system. All open positions are locked, and no new entries are permitted. Emergency manual review is required to reset.

## Active Risk Guards & Parameters

| Guard Name | Metric Evaluated | Nominal Limit | Action on Trigger | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Spread Guard** | `spread_bps` | `< spread_limit_bps` (Default: 5.0) | `SOFT_BLOCK` | Prevents entries during high-spread periods (e.g., news releases or rollover). |
| **Slippage Guard** | `slippage_bps` | `< slippage_limit_bps` (Default: 3.0) | `SOFT_BLOCK` | Prevents entry during low-liquidity states to minimize execution drag. |
| **ATR Shock Guard** | `atr_ratio` | `< atr_ratio_limit` (Default: 2.0) | `SOFT_BLOCK` | Disables trading during extreme price spikes (volatility expansion). |
| **Daily Drawdown Guard**| `daily_dd_pct` | `< daily_loss_limit_pct` (Default: 1.8% or 2.0%) | `SOFT_BLOCK` | Stops trading for the day to preserve capital during market shifts. |
| **Weekly Drawdown Guard**| `weekly_dd_pct`| `< weekly_soft_stop_pct` (Default: 4.0%) | `SOFT_BLOCK` | Halts weekly trading when accumulated losses mount. |
| **Loss Streak Guard** | `loss_streak` | `< loss_streak_pause` (Default: 3 trades) | `SOFT_BLOCK` | Pauses execution daily after consecutive losses to prevent overtrading. |
| **Hard Drawdown Guard** | `daily_dd` / `weekly_dd` | `< hard_drawdown_pct` (Default: 8.0%) | `HARD_KILL` | Absolute safety circuit-breaker to stop catastrophic system failures. |
| **Latency Guard** | Time difference | `< timeout_minutes` (Default: 120m) | `SOFT_BLOCK` | Discards stale signals if the processing or connection delay is too high. |
